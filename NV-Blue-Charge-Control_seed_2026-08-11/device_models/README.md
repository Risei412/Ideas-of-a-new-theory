# Device models

Reserved for the first device-level closure.

Planned inputs:
- measured/credible hyperfine tensors for at least two nuclear spins;
- charge-state-dependent effective nuclear Hamiltonians for NV- and NV0;
- MW/RF control amplitudes and bandwidths;
- blue-light charge preparation / ionization / recombination rates;
- charge readout confusion matrix;
- charge dwell-time / timestamp resolution;
- electron and nuclear decoherence during the coherent window.

Do not treat the synthetic four-level coefficients in `calculations/` as fitted NV device parameters.
