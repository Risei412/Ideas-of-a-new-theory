# Primary literature anchors

These are the primary experimental/theory anchors used when constructing the seed. Re-check exact device parameters before manuscript use.

- Waldherr et al., Physical Review Letters 106, 157601 (2011): coherent nuclear-spin control in neutral NV charge state.
- Bradley et al., npj Quantum Information 8, 122 (2022), “Robust quantum-network memory based on spin qubits in isotopically engineered diamond”: heralded NV- -> NV0 -> NV- switching, charge-dependent nuclear evolution, nuclear memory across charge cycles.
- Bradley et al., work on multi-nuclear-spin NV registers and coherent control: establishes the broader nuclear-register control substrate.
- Völker et al., Nano Letters 24, 11895–11903 (2024), DOI 10.1021/acs.nanolett.4c03064: UV/blue excitation, including 445 nm, modifies NV charge-state preparation and can destabilize shallow NV charge/spin states.
- Baier et al., Physical Review Letters 125, 193601 (2020): NV0 orbital/spin dynamics.
- Shields et al., Physical Review Letters 114, 136402 (2015): spin-to-charge conversion via spin-dependent photoionization.

Novelty boundary references for TP-16 include general repeat-until-success, quantum-trajectory/unravelling and measurement-feedback frameworks. Those general ideas are explicitly not claimed as new here.
