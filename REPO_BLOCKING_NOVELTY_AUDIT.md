## Closest absorbers and what they kill

### Linear quantum stochastic transfer realization
Grivopoulos, Nurdin, Petersen and related LQSS realization work construct physical quantum realizations of prescribed transfer functions, including cascade/feedback architectures.

**Kills:** novelty based on realizing a desired detector transfer function.

### Power-spectrum identification
Levitt, Guță, Nurdin study identification and realization from stationary output power spectra, with transfer-function recovery up to symplectic equivalence in the relevant minimal setting.

**Kills:** novelty based on output covariance/power spectrum being an unusual identification object.

### Gaussian-state QFI
General Gaussian QFI formulae explicitly depend on both first-moment and covariance tangents.

**Kills:** novelty based on “same baseline covariance can have different QFI when the parameter tangent differs.”

### Finite-frequency response-noise-QFI bounds
Gu and Liu (arXiv:2605.03340, 2026) derive detector-facing finite-frequency response-to-noise inequalities bounded by output-field QFI rate, with a further activity bound for dissipative amplitude modulation.

**Kills:** novelty based on the existence of response/noise/QFI inequalities.

**Open residue:** whether those necessary bounds, together with physical realizability, are sufficient; if not, identify the missing constraint and construct minimal realizations of the boundary.

### Hermitian compression / higher-rank numerical range
For fixed hidden damping Gram matrix, `C_h = U Gamma_h^(1/2)` gives

`Gamma_h^(-1/2) K_h Gamma_h^(-1/2) = U^† N_h U`.

Hermitian compression and interlacing/inverse-compression theory are established matrix analysis.

**Kills:** novelty based on the compression body itself.

### Reservoir engineering
Passive/linear reservoir engineering, including anisotropic or squeezed thermal reservoirs, is established and active.

**Kills:** novelty based on “environment orientation can engineer noise.”

## Surviving repository target

The candidate must contain **all** of the following:

1. exact fixed complete detector-facing baseline response;
2. fixed declared baseline output noise object;
3. fixed signal grammar/resource accounting;
4. characterization of the physically realizable signal-tangent / output-information image;
5. a new constraint, obstruction, completeness theorem, or minimal constructive synthesis result not reducible to standard matrix compression or transfer realization.

## Internal collision boundary

Notion already contains image-side realizability programs (RFFB and Routing-Contrast Realizability Bounds). This repository must not claim that “studying reachable sets” is new. Its orthogonal primitive is **fixed-response/noise operational synthesis of parameterized open-system tangents**, rather than memory-dimension Fisher-fan bounds or classical-versus-coherent detector-routing separation.
